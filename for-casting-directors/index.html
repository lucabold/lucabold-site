Work eligibility

Italian and South African passports.

The Italian passport carries EU citizenship — the right to live and work in all 27 EU member states and the EEA. For a production in Milan, Paris, Berlin or Amsterdam that means no work permit, no sponsorship cost and no visa lead time. The South African passport covers work across Southern Africa.

Ten years of resident working experience across Asia, including Shanghai, Hong Kong, Seoul, Mumbai and Bali.

Availability

Cape Town-based for the full Southern Hemisphere production season, October 2026 – February 2027 — local rates apply. A production booking him in the Western Cape during this window treats him as a local hire. Available for travel to other markets during the period by arrangement.
