Booking Questions

Is Luca Bold available in Cape Town?

Yes — Cape Town from October 2026 to February 2027, the full Southern Hemisphere production season. He is a local hire for any production shooting in the Western Cape, so there is no travel, accommodation or per diem cost. He also holds Italian (EU) and South African passports, so an international production booking him carries no permit or visa lead time. Enquiries through ICE Models, Cape Town.

Does Luca Bold require a work permit or visa sponsorship?

No permit or sponsorship is required anywhere in the EU or EEA. Luca holds an Italian passport and is an EU citizen with the right to work in all 27 member states. He also holds a South African passport. For EU productions there is no visa lead time, no sponsorship cost and no scheduling risk.

What kind of work does he take?

Campaign and commercial: advertising stills, TVC and campaign film, luxury and lifestyle. Active sectors are luxury watches, technology, grooming and skincare, beverage and apparel, with automotive, corporate and financial lifestyle, and premium travel and hospitality actively being built. Runway and editorial are taken selectively.

Which markets has he worked in?

Milan, Shanghai, Cape Town, Hong Kong, Seoul, Bali, Mumbai and Istanbul — eight markets over ten years, with agency representation in each. He travels internationally for bookings worldwide.

How are fees and image usage structured?

The shoot fee and the image licence are quoted separately, on the standard advertising model. Usage is priced against territory, media and term — so a national one-year print licence and a worldwide three-year all-media licence are different conversations. Send the brief to ICE Models and a quote follows.

How do you book him?

All modelling enquiries go through ICE Models, Cape Town — mother agency, representing him worldwide. His booking agent is Raymond Weston — raymond@icemodels.co.za, +27 (0)82 777 3302. The Asia scouting desk is Cat — cat.asiascout@gmail.com. Enquiries about Booked or the Get Signed Accelerator are a separate business and go direct to Luca.
