Booked

Your work is good.
The systems can’t read it.

Casting directors, brands and AI tools find talent by searching. A portfolio that looks good to a human but is unreadable to a machine doesn’t get surfaced. Most creatives have no idea this is happening to them.

What it actually costs.

A casting director searches for a male model in Cape Town who has shot watch campaigns. Your portfolio has the work. But the images have no metadata, the site has no structured data, and the booking route is a generic contact form buried three clicks deep.

You don’t get shortlisted. Not because you’re wrong for the job — because you were never in the results.

This is not a niche problem. It is how the industry works now. Platforms, AI recommendation systems and search engines read structure, not talent. If your work is not structured, it is invisible to the systems handing out the opportunities.

What Booked Does

Make your existing work readable.

Booked is not an agency. It does not replace your agent or your portfolio. It makes what you already have legible to the systems that surface talent.

01

Infrastructure Score

An audit of your digital presence — how visible you are to AI systems, casting platforms and search. Where you are strong, where you are leaking opportunities, and exactly what to fix.

02

Portfolio Infrastructure

Structured data, image metadata, schema markup. The layer between your work and the machines reading it. Built once, compounds over time.

03

Booking Architecture

Direct routes to your agent or to you — not a contact form. Every booking path surfaced, labelled and machine-readable.
