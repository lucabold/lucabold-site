    Questions

    Do I have to give my email to see the result?

    No. The full result — your band, the per-area breakdown and all three fixes — is shown on screen before any email field appears. The email option exists only if you want a copy sent to you, and the page works identically if you ignore it.

    Is anything stored?

    Nothing. Your answers exist only in the page's memory while you have it open. There is no account, no cookie, no local storage, and no analytics on this page. Reloading the page erases everything and starts over, which is by design.

    How long does it take?

    About three minutes. Twelve questions, one at a time, and you can go back and change an answer before you finish.

    How accurate is a twelve-question self-assessment?

    It is a triage instrument, not a diagnosis. It is good at finding which of the four areas is weakest, which is the useful output, and it is limited by the fact that you are scoring yourself on things you cannot see. The area people consistently over-rate themselves on is consistency across images, because they have never looked at their images as a set.

    Why are the fixes this detailed?

    Because a result that only tells you that you have a problem is worth nothing. Each fix is written to be executed this week without hiring anyone. If you never enquire, you should still leave with something that changes an outcome.
