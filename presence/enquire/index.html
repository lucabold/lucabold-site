Enquire about presence advisory

Send the form below with what is coming up and when. You get a reply within two working days — either a quote against your situation, or a note telling you this is the wrong service for your problem.

The more specific the dates and situations, the more useful the reply. An enquiry saying “raising a Series A, first partner meetings 4 November, London and Munich” can be quoted immediately; one saying “want to look better” cannot.

The form

If you would rather see a result before speaking to anyone, the twelve-question diagnostic is free, needs no email to read, and returns three specific fixes.

Before you send it

Two things make a reply materially more useful, and both take a minute.

Name the trigger. Almost nobody enquires about this in the abstract. There is usually a specific thing — a first institutional round, a keynote, a board seat, a new photograph needed for a site that goes live in three weeks. Name it and the reply is about that rather than about generalities.

Say what you are actually worried about. Not the polished version. The useful answers to that field tend to be things like “I look about ten years younger than I am and it is costing me in rooms with institutional investors” or “I have no idea whether what I wear is fine or embarrassing and nobody will tell me”. That is the real brief, and it is the field most worth spending two extra minutes on.

What happens with each engagement, what you send and what comes back, is set out on how it works.

Questions

What happens after I send this?

You get a reply within two working days, which is either a quote against what you have described or a note saying this is not the right service for your problem. There is no sequence of follow-up emails and no call booked before there is something specific to discuss.

Do I need to know which tier I want?

No. Describe the situation and the date and the right tier is usually obvious from that. If a strategy session would resolve it, that is what gets proposed rather than something larger.

Is there a discovery call first?

Only where the scope is genuinely unclear. Most enquiries can be quoted from the form, and a call before that is a cost to both of us with no information in it.

What if my timeline is very short?

Say so in the form and give the date. A compressed version built backwards from a fixed deadline is possible, and if it is not possible I will say that rather than take the work and under-deliver on it.

Is what I send confidential?

Yes. Nothing about who I work with is published, no names or images are used as examples anywhere, and there are no testimonials or case studies on this site — there is nothing to opt into.

Background, campaign credits and representation are on the main site. Presence advisory is a separate practice from modelling bookings, which go through the agency desk listed there.
